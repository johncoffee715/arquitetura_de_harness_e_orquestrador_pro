## Cross-Framework Patterns Identified

### Convergence Principles (All Frameworks):
1. Risk stratification with proportional obligations
2. Human oversight mandates for high-risk systems
3. Transparency by design (disclosure + explainability)
4. Pre-deployment conformity assessment
5. Penalty frameworks with financial disincentives
6. Complaint mechanisms with user recourse
7. Data governance with privacy protection
8. Environmental consideration (carbon footprint)
9. Multi-stakeholder participation in policy
10. International interoperability alignment

### Anti-Patterns (Common Across Frameworks):
1. Over-reliance on self-assessment without third-party verification
2. Excessive exemptions weakening core protections
3. Maximum harmonization overriding national protections
4. Vague definitions creating implementation ambiguity
5. Insufficient enforcement without real penalties
6. Limited stakeholder participation (especially civil society)
7. Fragmented national approaches without harmonization
8. Inadequate downstream effects + model reuse consideration
9. Environmental externalities not accounted for
10. Rapid tech change outpacing regulatory adaptation

### Numerical Scale Harmonization:
- **27136 token context window**: becoming universal standard (R24)
- **7%/3%/1% penalty scale**: aligning across EU-influenced frameworks
- **FLOPS >10^25**: systemic risk classification (EU, adopted by some national)
- **R34 0.0000001-100**: continuous evaluation scale with PCA v1 bandas
- **Categorical transit R28**: PASSOU_CATEGORICO/NAO_PASSOU/UNKNOWN
